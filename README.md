# Levi Woodard

Compute at **Akamai** (formerly Linode). Volunteer firefighter in western South Dakota. Founder of **[The Woodard Foundation](https://woodard.foundation)**, a nonprofit that secures the tools and systems volunteer fire departments depend on.

I spend my days on cloud infrastructure and my nights and weekends on fire calls and the technology behind them. Most of what ends up here is the overlap: small, practical tooling for people who run systems without a team behind them.

## What I work on

- **Infrastructure and operations.** Linux, Kubernetes, Docker, Ansible, and the unglamorous work of keeping things running.
- **Security for small organizations.** Account hardening, backups, station networks, and incident response for volunteer departments and the nonprofits that support them.
- **Tooling.** Go and Python utilities, shell scripts, and Hugo sites. If I've had to do something three times, there's probably a script for it in [`scripts`](https://github.com/WoodardDigital/scripts).

## Around here

- [`piscreen`](https://github.com/WoodardDigital/piscreen) turns a Raspberry Pi into a dedicated UniFi camera display.
- [`tasks`](https://github.com/WoodardDigital/tasks) is a task manager written in Go.
- [`herdr-remote`](https://github.com/WoodardDigital/herdr-remote) adds integrated remote sessions to Herdr.
- [`Monarch-Money-Walmart`](https://github.com/WoodardDigital/Monarch-Money-Walmart) splits Walmart receipts into itemized Monarch Money transactions.
